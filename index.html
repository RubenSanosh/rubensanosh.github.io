About

I'm a Grade 11 student in Edmonton with a growing interest in IT and cybersecurity. Most of what I know so far comes from experimenting on my own rather than formal coursework. I run a home server on Ubuntu Server that hosts a media library, a Minecraft server for friends, and a small AI agent I built to automate a few repetitive tasks. On my personal machine I use Arch Linux, which has taught me a lot about how Linux systems actually work under the hood.

Outside of technology, most of my experience comes from community work: organizing events at St. Alphonsa Syro Malabar Church, volunteering at the Edmonton Food Bank, and coaching youth soccer through the YMCA. These roles taught me how to manage responsibilities, communicate clearly, and follow through on tasks, skills I'm now applying to learning Linux administration, networking, and cloud fundamentals.

Right now: I'm focused on building a stronger technical foundation through self-directed projects, with the goal of pursuing formal education and a career in IT or cybersecurity.
